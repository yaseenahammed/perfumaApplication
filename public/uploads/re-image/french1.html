Unveil sophistication with French Essence Oud Royale Men’s Luxury Perfume – 100ml, a fragrance crafted for true connoisseurs.

Opening with the spicy warmth of saffron and nutmeg blended with lavender, this perfume transitions into the deep richness of agarwood (oud) at its heart. The journey ends with a luxurious base of musk and patchouli, leaving behind an irresistible and long-lasting trail.

Encased in a premium designer bottle, Oud Royale is more than a scent - it’s a statement of elegance and power. Perfect for both daily wear and special occasions, this unisex oud fragrance delivers luxury, depth, and timeless charm.

Step into the royal aura and elevate your fragrance game.

NOTES

  • Top Notes: Saffron & Lavender
  • Middle Notes: Oud
  • Base Notes: Musk & Patchouli

HOW TO USE

  • Apply the perfume to pulse points, like the sides of your neck and wrists, for long-lasting freshness throughout the day.
